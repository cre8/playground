    What happens here?

    Professor Oak issues your starter EUDIMON as a credential (SD-JWT VC) using the pre-authorized code flow: no login, just scan the QR code, optionally with a PIN.

    • Each starter has its own credential config, so it gets its own card in your wallet.
    • The four moves are separately disclosable claims: move_1 to move_4.
    • In the arena against Gary, each move is disclosed only when you use it for the first time.
