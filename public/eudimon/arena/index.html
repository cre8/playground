    Under the hood

      What happens here?

      The arena is a verifier, and every disclosure is a separate presentation request:

      • eudimon-battle sends out your EUDIMON: species, nickname and level.
      • eudimon-move-1 to eudimon-move-4 ask for exactly one move, plus species and nickname to check it is still the same EUDIMON.
      • A move Gary already knows needs no new request.

      DCQL could let the wallet choose (credential_sets, claim_sets), but today's wallets answer with the first matching option. So you choose on the page, and each request has exactly one answer. Your trainer name and ID are never requested. No EUDIMON yet? Get one at Professor Oak's lab.
